What this page will surface
Engage / panic-release
A primary toggle to hand control to the policy, plus a one-key hard release that floors the gas at zero and recenters the wheel.
Live preview
MJPEG stream of the captured frame with the segmentation overlay and detection boxes from the active perception model.
Decision readout
Current steer, throttle, brake values, the target lane, and the chosen rule (or PPO policy head) that produced them.
Safety rails
Connection state for the virtual pad, off-road / wall detection, and an auto-disengage when the model is uncertain.
